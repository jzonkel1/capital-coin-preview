About the shop

The coin shop under the same roof as Afram's Jewelers.

Capital Coin has been buying and selling coins on Airline Road since 2014. Collectors stop in every week to see what's new in the cases, and there's usually more behind the counter if you ask.

Families bring in a parent's coins, a jar of old silver or a box of bills from the attic. We go through it with you, piece by piece, and you leave knowing what it's worth.
