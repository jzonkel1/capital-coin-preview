About the shop

Collectors come back every week.

Capital Coin has been buying and selling coins on Airline Road since 2014. It's a family business, part of the Afram family's three shops at 1701 Airline Road, alongside Afram's Jewelers and Modern Pawn & Guns.

Collectors stop in every week to see what's new in the cases, and families bring in a parent's coins, a jar of old silver or a box of bills from the attic. Either way, we go through it with you, piece by piece, and you leave knowing what it's worth.

We're a PCGS and NGC Platinum Member dealer, we pay on the spot during most deals, and for larger estates we'll come to you.
