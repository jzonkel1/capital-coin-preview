Paper money

That old bill might be worth more than its face.

Silver certificates, red seal notes, large old bills from a grandparent's drawer, foreign money from someone's travels: paper money is one of the things families bring in most.

We look each note up with you, explain what makes it worth more or less (the series, the condition, the serial number), and make a fair offer if you'd like to sell. Some notes are worth face value, and some are worth a great deal more.

Don't flatten, clean or tape old bills before you bring them in. Leave them the way you found them.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Is my old bill worth more than face value?

It might be. It depends on the series, the condition and sometimes the serial number. Bring it in and we'll look it up with you.

Should I clean or flatten old money?

No. Bring it in as it is. Cleaning, pressing or taping a note can lower its value.

Do you buy foreign money?

Bring it in and we'll take a look. Some foreign notes are collectible.

Can I text a photo first?

Yes. Text both sides to (361) 334-4926.
